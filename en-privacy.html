Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits to and use of our website. Information collected may include page views, session activity, approximate location, browser and device information, scrolling, outbound link clicks, site searches, video engagement and file downloads.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, such as _ga and _ga_<container-id>, to distinguish visitors and sessions. Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization are disabled.

For details of how Google handles this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Cookie settings

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking analytics cookies may limit the information available about website use.

Handling booking inquiries received through Gmail

We use read-only access to the Gmail API to review booking inquiries received by AIRE SPA's business Gmail account. We check the sender's name and address, subject, body preview, time received and message identifier for new emails, and classify them as booking inquiries, emails requiring review or emails unrelated to bookings.

Only emails classified as booking inquiries are displayed as notifications to the staff responsible for reservations. Read-only access is not used to send, modify or delete emails, and does not change their read status in Gmail. Processed information is accessible only to authorized administrators and reservation staff.

Authentication credentials are managed as server secrets in Cloudflare, and classified inquiry information is stored in Supabase. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
